### Overall Copy Problems

1. **Dangerous Tier-Boundary Contradictions (CRM & Third-Party Costs):** 
   The copy advertises CRM routing on the entry-level £49/month "Hazır" otomasyon page ("tabloya / CRM'e işlenir"), directly contradicting the package comparison table and FAQ which state CRM connections require the £149/month "Bağlantılı" tier. Furthermore, third-party messaging fees (Twilio/WhatsApp Business API) are concealed on the main pricing table, only appearing in fine print on subpages.

2. **Context-Blind Component Duplication:**
   Interactive widget modules are duplicated across subpages without contextual adaptation. The `/otomasyon/talep-yakalama` (lead capture) page displays a haircut salon appointment booking flow (`Salon randevusu · 21:14 · saç kesimi`), severely undermining credibility for B2B tradesmen, property agents, and consultants browsing that specific solution.

3. **Enterprise & Industrial Credibility Gap:**
   The site attempts to pitch high-ticket £2,950+ complex automations to industrial factories, distributors, and ERP operators, but backs this claim solely with coffee shop, preschool, and food truck portfolio websites. Highlighting that a single founder handles all architecture, execution, and ongoing support without secondary escalation or SLA backups will alarm corporate decision-makers wary of single-point-of-failure risks.

4. **Breaches of the "Zero Fluff & Verifiable Claims" Standard:**
   Despite an explicitly stated strategy of absolute transparency, the copy relies on untracked, legally vulnerable operational claims such as *"Genelde aynı gün dönüyoruz"* (repeated four times) and *"birkaç gün içinde çalışsın"*. These should be replaced with strict, professional SLA turnaround ranges.

5. **Linguistic Artifacts and Translation Drift:**
   The Turkish copy exhibits literal translation patterns and grammatical flaws: broken idioms (*"14 gün para iade"* instead of proper case inflection), literal mistranslations (*"form veya kutu"* for mailbox), semantic ambiguity (*"GELİR"* as a step label meaning revenue rather than incoming enquiry), and vowel harmony errors (*"£399’den"* instead of *"£399'dan"*).
