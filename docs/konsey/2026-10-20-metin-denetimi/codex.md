- The approval promise needs one site-wide rule. Create a simple internal matrix defining which messages require case-by-case approval and which fixed messages may run automatically after written authorisation.
- Commercial terms need a single source of truth: refund scope, minimum-term consequences, annual hosting, Custom contract length, third-party charges and integration limits.
- Capability is repeatedly written as a guarantee: “every,” “instantly,” “within days,” “same day,” “gets found” and “works with whatever you use” should be qualified or supported by measured evidence.
- The homepage and service pages over-repeat the founder paragraph, “honestly tell you” CTA and next-step block. `/ureticiler` even answers “Who will we work with?” immediately before repeating the full founder section.
- Standardise terminology: Turkish should consistently use `e-posta`, `kurulum`, `talep`, `işlem`, and `katılım/iptal yanıtı` rather than mixing `mail`, `setup`, `onay` and overlapping meanings. English CTAs should use “Message us on WhatsApp,” not “Write on WhatsApp.”
