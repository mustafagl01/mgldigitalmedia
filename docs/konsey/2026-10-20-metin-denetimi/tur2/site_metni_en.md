Frequently asked questions
Which plan is right for me?
Look at the tools you use, not the size of your company. Excel, WhatsApp and a calendar: Ready-made. A CRM or accounting software to connect: Connected. ERP, document reading or multilingual work: Complex.
We are a small business. Is this for us?
Yes. Ready-made automations are designed for small businesses: pick one from the list and it is set up at a fixed price. You can move up as your needs grow.
What if I need something that is not on the list?
Anything outside the ready-made list, or anything that connects several tasks, falls under Connected. Describe it and we will tell you honestly which plan it fits.
What counts as a "task"?
One piece of work the system handles end to end: a reminder, an enquiry, an order or a document. Follow-up messages on the same item are not counted again.
Do we have to change our software?
Usually not. We first check how your current tools can connect; the method, or any workaround needed, is written into the scope.
What if I want changes later?
Small fixes within scope are included in the monthly fee. New workflows or out-of-scope changes are done at a price agreed in writing first.
Which task takes up most of your time?
Describe it in a few lines. We will tell you honestly whether it can be automated and which plan it fits.
