- Standardise the product names as “AI telefon asistanı” and “AI WhatsApp asistanı”; avoid switching between “Sesli Asistan,” “telefon asistanı” and other variants.
- Use “e-posta” consistently instead of alternating between “mail” and “e-posta.”
- Keep all buttons in the formal plural imperative: “Yazın,” “Planlayın,” “Onaylayın,” “Düzenleyin.”
- Short headline fragments work, but retain a verb whenever removing it creates ambiguity or makes the copy resemble internal notes.
- Create one canonical version of the repeated demo disclaimer and final CTA, then reuse it unchanged across all pages.
