### Overall Voice & Style Feedback

1. **Eliminate English Copywriting Tropes (Staccato Fragments & "Calques"):** Phrases like *"Küçük bir şirket. Bilerek."* (*"A small company. By design."*) and *"Yazılı olarak."* (*"In writing."*) read like literal English translations. Turkish commercial communication values complete sentences with dignified, active verbs that convey stability and competence.
2. **Remove Street Vernacular & Defensive Phrasing:** Expressions like *"Uydurma yok"*, *"yanmaz"*, and *"beğenirseniz devam"* undercut technical credibility. Both a high-precision factory director and a local salon owner look for seriousness and reassurance; use words like *"Şeffaflık"*, *"devreder"*, and *"memnun kalırsanız"*.
3. **Purge Internal IT / Developer Jargon:** Replace software-shop buzzwords (*"canlıya alındığı gün"*, *"varsayılan olarak"*, *"otomasyon tarafında"*) with standard operational Turkish (*"devreye alındığı gün"*, *"öncelikli olarak"*, *"otomasyon çözümlerimizde"*).
4. **Standardize AI Terminology:** The copy fluctuates between *"AI telefon ve WhatsApp asistanı"*, *"asistan"*, and *"model eğitimi"*. Standardize consistently on **"AI asistan"** across all headings and body text, keeping it distinct from custom backend automations.
5. **Enforce Parallelism & Action-Oriented CTAs:** Several button labels and section subheadings use passive noun clusters (*"15 dakikalık görüşme"*, *"Dört ilke."*, *"Sizin araçlarınız"*). Convert all primary buttons to clear, courteous imperative verbs (*"Görüşme Planlayın"*, *"Bize Yazın"*) and section titles to balanced descriptive headings.
