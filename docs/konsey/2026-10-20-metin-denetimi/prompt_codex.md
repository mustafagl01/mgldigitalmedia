You are reviewing ALL visible text of a bilingual (Turkish + English) business website before it goes live. Be strict and concrete. Disagree openly. Do not invent facts.

Facts that must stay consistent everywhere: Ready-made 14,900 TRY / £349 setup + 1,490 TRY / £49 per month, 3-month minimum, live demo + 14-day money-back; Connected 35,000 TRY / £950 + 4,900 / £149, 6-month minimum, free 14-day trial; Complex from 100,000 TRY / £2,950 + from 9,900 / £399, 12-month minimum; Custom quoted. Prices shown on the site depend on the visitor's region (the text below was captured with UK prices).

## What to check
1. Readability and clarity: sentences that are hard to understand, too long, jargon, vague marketing filler.
2. Turkish quality: unnatural or translated-sounding Turkish, grammar, wrong suffixes, inconsistent terms (e.g. "talep" vs "işlem", "kurulum" vs "setup"). English quality likewise.
3. Contradictions between pages: prices, minimum terms, trial/refund wording, what is included, counts.
4. Claims that could be untrue or unverifiable ("usually same day", "within days", etc.) — flag them.
5. Repetition: the same sentence or idea repeated too often across pages.
6. Anything a small-business owner OR a factory director would not understand or would distrust.

Privacy and security
You stay in control.
In writing.
What the system does on its own, what it leaves to you and where your data lives. Full legal text: Legal.
Approval points
Every message, price and quote to your customers waits for your approval by default.
Automatic sending is only switched on at your written request, for fixed messages you choose.
The system never makes pricing, technical or legal decisions.
Your data
Your data is processed only for the agreed work and is never used to train AI models.
Files stay in your own accounts (Google Drive, OneDrive, ERP) wherever possible.
We sign an NDA on request; GDPR and KVKK terms are written into the proposal.
If needed, the whole system runs on your own server or account.
Trial and refund
Ready-made: live demo first; if you are not happy within 14 days of setup, the setup fee is refunded.
Connected: one workflow on your own data, free for 14 days; continue with the setup fee if you like it.
Complex: the free 14-day trial covers one document type or one process; success criteria are written first.
No automatic switch to a paid plan. If you stop, trial data is deleted.
Ownership and cancellation
Websites are a one-off purchase and belong to you.
Minimum terms: Ready-made 3 months, Connected 6, Complex 12; the term starts on go-live.
Ads management and assistants are monthly; ad spend is paid directly to the platform.
