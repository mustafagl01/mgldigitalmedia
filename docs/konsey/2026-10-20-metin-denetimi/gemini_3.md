### Overall Copy Problems

* **Severe Factual Contradiction on AI Assistants & Pricing:** `/gizlilik-ve-guvenlik` states that assistant services are billed monthly ("asistanlar aylıktır"), directly contradicting the non-negotiable business model where assistant credit bundles never expire. Furthermore, it omits the first-year free hosting included with websites and invents an unverified 14-day free trial for the high-ticket Complex automation tier.
* **Omission of Core Services in Key Sections:** On `/hakkimizda`, the company’s summary only lists websites, ads, and automation—completely leaving out AI phone and WhatsApp assistants, which is one of the four flagship offerings.
* **Monotonous and Context-Deaf CTA Duplication:** The identical automation-centric block (*"En çok vaktinizi alan iş hangisi? ... Otomatikleştirilebilir mi..."*) is copy-pasted across `/hakkimizda`, `/gizlilik-ve-guvenlik`, and `/isler`. Placing a pushy automation pitch on a legal/compliance page or on a portfolio page displaying 12 websites alienates non-automation leads.
* **Unverifiable Response Claims & Single-Operator Friction:** Promising *"genelde aynı gün dönüyoruz"* and claiming *"mesai dışında da telefondan bakıyoruz"* sets unsustainable, legally unverifiable SLA expectations for a one-person business. Simultaneously, emphasizing that the founder is the sole person answering calls and maintaining servers introduces severe key-person risk for factory directors and enterprise buyers who require operational continuity.
* **Tone Shifts and Structural Fragments:** The Turkish copy alternates abruptly between raw colloquialisms (*"telefondan bakıyoruz"*, *"sitede yazılı"*, *"yazılı netleşir"*) and incomplete, verbless sentence fragments (*"Yasal metnin tamamı: Yasal."*, *"Sistemin neyi kendi yaptığı..."*), detracting from the company's credibility.
