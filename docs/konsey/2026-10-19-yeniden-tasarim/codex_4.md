## 1. Is the current hero suitable?

**No.** I disagree with keeping it as shipped.

The four tiles technically name every service, but the dominant signals—headline, blue routing lines, green approval node and automation doors—all say “automation company.” On mobile, the tiles also consume most of the first screen without creating a clear narrative. Websites and ads feel appended rather than central.

## 2. Final messaging

Keep **one umbrella promise**, not a headline that lists four services. The customer journey is the right umbrella; approval should remain the closing control promise, not the entire concept.

**EN H1**  
**Get found. Never miss an enquiry. Let the work run—with your approval.**

**EN lede**  
Websites and ads bring customers in; AI phone and WhatsApp assistants respond; automation handles the follow-up—nothing customer-facing happens without your approval.

**TR H1**  
**Müşteriler sizi bulsun. Hiçbir talep kaçmasın. İşler onayınızla yürüsün.**

**TR lede**  
Web siteleri ve reklamlar müşteriyi getirir; AI telefon ve WhatsApp asistanları yanıtlar; otomasyon devamını yürütür—müşteriye giden hiçbir şey onayınız olmadan çıkmaz.

The approval metaphor still works, but as the **final safety promise across the journey**. It should not visually dominate every service.

## 3. First-screen structure

Limit the hero to five elements:

1. Audience tag: UK + Türkiye · Small businesses to manufacturers  
2. Journey-based H1  
3. One-line lede  
4. CTA group: **Find the right service** + **Talk on WhatsApp**  
5. Journey image  

Remove both the service tiles and automation doors from the hero.

Immediately after the hero:

- **“Four ways we help”** — the four service cards, each clearly available individually.
- Then the **approval simulator**, introduced as the automation service.
- Put the two doors—**Small business** and **Manufacturer or distributor**—inside the simulator’s introduction.

So, **no**, the simulator should not sit directly under the hero. A short four-service overview must come first; otherwise automation immediately takes over the page again.

## 4. Hero image

**Regenerate it.** The current image successfully communicates connected operations, but not discovery, advertising or enquiry handling.

Create a premium white miniature business journey moving from right to centre: a search/result or illuminated storefront module, then a restrained phone/message reception module, followed by cobalt workflow rails carrying the enquiry through documents and operational tasks to one green approval desk. For desktop 16:9, keep the left 45% quiet and low-detail for the copy, with the journey concentrated across the right half. For mobile 9:16, leave the upper area empty for text and arrange the same journey vertically downward, ending at the green approval point near the bottom.

Avoid floating app icons or dashboard-style graphics; it should remain architectural and physical.

## 5. Main risk

The integrated journey may imply that customers must buy all four services together. Counter this immediately below the hero by stating: **“Start with one service, or connect the whole journey.”**
