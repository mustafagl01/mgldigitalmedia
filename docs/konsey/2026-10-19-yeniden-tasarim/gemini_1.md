### 8. What Would Make This Redesign Fail (Open Disagreement)
1. **Trying to sound like an agency of 50 people:** If the copy says "Our global team of engineers," an operations director doing a 30-second background check on Companies House will see a solo director and bounce immediately (under 5 seconds, estimate). Lean fully into the solo founder's agility, personal accountability, and phone-managed oversight.
2. **Diluting cold B2B traffic with salon templates:** Forcing a manufacturing executive who clicked a cold email to read through salon booking demos will kill the lead. Highlighting modular workflow tiers based on operational complexity—rather than consumer niches—is the only way both audiences can coexist without contradiction.
